First audio is measured twice: in this page, from pressing Speak to the first frame arriving, and by the server from its own clock. They differ by the network, and on a remote host that difference is real latency you would also see in a call.

#SentenceCharsAudio ExpectedFirst audio Ended onPad eos ignored

Expected is the server's own reading-speed assumption (guard_chars_per_second). A clip under half of it is flagged short — that is what a dropped sentence looks like. Ended on is the server's finish_reason: end_of_speech is the model closing the audio span properly, text_eos is the backbone's own end token, max_tokens means it never stopped and hit the cap.
