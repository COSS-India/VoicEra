VoicEra follows the code of conduct in [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/COSS-India/VoicEra/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) at the repository root. This page reproduces it for convenience; the repository file is authoritative.

## Our commitment

We want VoicEra to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual identity and orientation, background, or affiliation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and considerate in speech and actions.
* Welcome newcomers and help them get oriented.
* Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
* Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or discrimination in any form.
* Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting or derogatory comments.
* Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a phone number, email or physical address, without their explicit permission.
* Threats of violence, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
* Sustained disruption of discussions or project spaces.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and any other official VoicEra channel — and to anyone officially representing the project in public, for example using an official project email address, posting through an official account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers at **TO BE FILLED BY K (conduct email)**.

* Include what happened, where (with links if possible), when, and who was involved. Screenshots help, because comments can be edited or deleted.
* You will receive an acknowledgement within **5 working days**.
* Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
* The maintainers keep the identity of the reporter confidential, and will not share details of a report without the reporter's consent except where required by law.
* If a report involves a maintainer, they will take no part in handling it.
* Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

You can also use GitHub's own tools at any time: block a user from your account, or report abusive content to GitHub directly from the comment's menu.

If you are in immediate danger, or the behaviour may be a crime, contact your local authorities first.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with it. When they do, they will explain the reason where appropriate.

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding on the consequences of a violation:

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** inappropriate language or other behaviour considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** a private, written warning from the maintainers, explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** a single serious incident, or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this Code of Conduct, for a specified period. This includes avoiding interaction in project spaces and in external channels such as social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact:** a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence:** a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact:** a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence:** a permanent ban from any public interaction within the project.

### Appeals

Anyone subject to an enforcement decision may appeal once, in writing, to the same address within 14 days. The appeal is reviewed by a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the project's leadership.
